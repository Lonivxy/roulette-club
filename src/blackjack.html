Table rules & payouts

Blackjack pays 3:2. Other wins pay 1:1; ties return your stake. Dealer stands on all 17s, including soft 17, and checks for blackjack on the deal. Double on any first two cards, including after a split. Split equal-value cards once into two hands. Split aces receive one card each. A split-hand 21 pays 1:1. No insurance or surrender. Bets: 10–1,000 chips, in steps of 10.

Winnings are credited to your player wallet. Leave and return to resume an unfinished hand.
